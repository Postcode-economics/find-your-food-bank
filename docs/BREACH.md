# Personal data breach procedure

A breach is any accidental or unlawful loss, disclosure, change or access to personal data, for example the hello@helpmap.uk mailbox or a form inbox being accessed by someone else, or a form message sent to the wrong person.

1. **Contain (same day).** Change passwords, revoke tokens, sign out other sessions, turn on 2FA, disable the affected form or workflow if needed.
2. **Record.** Write down what happened, when it was found, what data and roughly how many people are involved, and what you did. Keep this record even if you decide not to report.
3. **Assess risk.** Could it cause harm to someone (e.g. reveal that a person used a food bank or is in a refuge, or expose their contact details)?
4. **Report to the ICO within 72 hours** of becoming aware, if the breach is likely to put people's rights at risk: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/report-a-breach/ (or 0303 123 1113). If not all facts are known, report what you know and update later.
5. **Tell the people affected without undue delay** if the risk to them is high, in plain language, with what they can do.
6. **Review.** Note what will stop it happening again and update `docs/RUNBOOK.md` if needed.
