  • Crisis Skylight Birmingham
    Day centre
    Daytime only. No overnight bed.
    Suite 303, Rhubarb, 25 Heath Mill Lane, Digbeth
    Phone: 0121 348 7950
    Website

    Who it's for: People over the age of 16 who are experiencing homelessness, or immediately at risk of homelessness · Covers Birmingham and Solihull

    How to get in:

    Self-referral: Call 0121 348 7950 (lines open weekdays 9am to 5pm) or email birmingham@crisis.org.uk. If you are unable to contact them by phone or email, drop in on weekdays between 9am and 4pm (they may not be able to offer an appointment that day). First visit: use the intercom on Heath Mill Lane. Please contact before visiting if you can. Crisis is not an emergency service. For urgent housing support at the weekend or after 5pm, Birmingham City Council’s out-of-hours housing team is 0121 303 2296. Contact them by phone or email about other requirements, such as needing an interpreter.

    Wheelchair access: Wheelchair accessible

    Facilities: Skylights can help in finding a home and settling in, finding work and applying for jobs, and looking after your health and wellbeing., How we can help depends on your situation and needs. If another service can provide the support you need, we will refer you to them., Engagement and Assessment: immediate information, advice and guidance, then a plan to end homelessness, Coaching service: longer-term support to help people address what may be contributing to their homelessness and achieve their goals, Supported by clinical psychologists, housing law specialists, and volunteering and events teams, The building has accessible bathroom facilities and the bathrooms are gender neutral
