Privacy & data protection statement

Last updated 22 October 2026

Thank you for using helpmap.uk. This statement explains, in plain terms, what information is involved when you use the site and how it is handled under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

helpmap.uk is run by Dean Goff, trading as Postcode Economics. Contact: hello@helpmap.uk.

What we collect

  • No accounts and no cookies. You never need to sign up, and the site does not set cookies.
  • Visitor statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which gives us anonymous, aggregate figures (such as page views). It is cookie-free and is not used to identify you.
  • Searches. The postcode or place name you type is sent to postcodes.io to find its location. We do not store your searches. Your most recent search, and display settings such as language or light mode, are saved only in your own browser so the map can show them next time; you can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Your location. If you choose “Use my location”, your device works out where you are and the map uses it in your browser only. It is not sent to us.
  • Reports and service updates. If you use the “Report a problem” or “Add or update your service” forms, the details you enter (including a name or email address, if you choose to give them) are sent to us by email through FormSubmit, so that we can check and correct the map.
  • Emails. If you email hello@helpmap.uk, we use your message only to respond to you.

Lawful basis

We rely on our legitimate interests in running, securing and improving a free public information service. When you contact us, or send a report, we use your details in order to respond to you and act on your message.

Third parties

  • Cloudflare: delivers and protects the website and provides the anonymous statistics.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • postcodes.io: looks up the location of postcodes and place names.
  • Map tiles: the map images come from MapTiler, with Stadia Maps or OpenStreetMap used as a back-up if MapTiler is unavailable. Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address when they send map images to your browser.
  • FormSubmit: forwards the report and service-update forms to us by email.

Each of these providers handles information under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep information

We keep emails and form messages only for as long as needed to respond and to keep the map accurate, and then delete them. Information saved in your browser stays there until you clear it.

If something goes wrong

If a personal data breach happened that was likely to put people's rights at risk, we would report it to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and tell the people affected where required.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or erasure of information we hold about you, and to object to how we use it. To make a request, please email hello@helpmap.uk and we will respond as soon as we can, and in any case within one month.

Making a complaint

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, we would appreciate the chance to put it right first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
